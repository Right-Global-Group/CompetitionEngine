# Free Entry Route

> How to enter competitions by post without making a purchase.

---

## Overview

UK competition law requires that every competition offers an alternative free entry method. On Competition Engine this is a free postal entry route. Anyone can enter by post without buying tickets.

---

## How to Enter by Post

Send an unenclosed postcard (no envelope) via stamped mail to the address shown on the competition page under the **Free Postal Entry** tab.

Your postcard must clearly include:

- Your full name
- Your date of birth
- Your full postal address
- Your email address
- One contact phone number
- The ID of the competition you want to enter

All details must be legible. Illegible entries cannot be processed.

Only one entry is allowed per postcard. Sending multiple competition entries on the same postcard does not comply with the terms and conditions — each entry must be on a separate postcard.

---

## Where to Find the Postal Address

The postal address is shown on the **Free Postal Entry** tab on every competition page. Open any live competition, click the **Free Postal Entry** tab next to Paid Entry, and the full instructions and postal address will be shown.

---

## Free Entry and Your Account

If you submit a postal entry, you must have an active account on the platform at the time of posting. All details on the postcard must match your account — name, date of birth, address, and email.

---

## Multiple Entries Per Stamp

On some competitions where the ticket price is lower than the cost of a first or second class stamp, you may receive multiple entries per postcard to ensure the entry value at least equals the stamp cost. For example, if a ticket costs 49p and a stamp costs 87p, you would receive 2 entries per postcard sent. The exact multiple will be stated in the competition terms.

---

## Eligibility

Postal entries must be received before the competition's closing date. Late entries cannot be accepted. Entries that don't follow all the instructions above will be considered void.
