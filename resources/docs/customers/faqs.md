# FAQs

> Answers to common questions about using Competition Engine.

---

## Entering Competitions

**Do I need an account to enter?**
Yes. You must be registered and logged in to purchase tickets or use the free postal entry route via the website. Postal entries still require a matching account.

**How do I know which tickets I have?**
Go to **My Account → My Tickets**. All tickets across all competitions are listed there, along with your specific ticket numbers for each entry.

**Can I enter the same competition more than once?**
Yes, up to the competition's maximum tickets per customer limit. Once you reach that limit the entry panel on the competition page will let you know.

**Can I pick my own ticket number?**
Some competitions offer Pick Your Number mode. When available, a number grid replaces the slider and you can select from the remaining available numbers. Selected numbers are reserved for 5 minutes while you complete checkout.

**What happens if a competition I entered doesn't sell out?**
Competitions draw based on their end date or sell-through target depending on how they are set up. Check the competition page for the specific draw conditions.

## Basket & Payment

**Why were items removed from my basket?**
If a competition ends while you have it in your basket, it is automatically removed at checkout. A message tells you which items were affected.

**Can I use my wallet balance and a card together?**
Yes. If your wallet doesn't cover the full total, a split payment lets your wallet cover as much as it can with the remainder charged to card or PayPal.

**Is my payment secure?**
Yes. All card payments are processed through our payment provider using industry standard encryption.

**Can I get a refund?**
Ticket purchases are non-refundable once an order is confirmed. Please check the competition terms before purchasing.

## Winning & Prizes

**How will I know if I've won?**
You'll receive a notification on the site and an email to the address registered on your account. Instant wins are shown on the order confirmation page immediately after purchase.

**Where do I go to claim a prize?**
Physical and alternative prizes appear in **My Account → Claim Prizes** once they are ready for you to action.

**How long do I have to claim a prize?**
Contact our support team if you have concerns about a prize expiring — timelines vary depending on the prize type.

**Can I choose cash instead of a physical prize?**
Some prizes offer a cash alternative. If available, you will see a cash option alongside the physical prize on the Claim Prizes page. The amount is shown on the prize card.

## Wallet

**How long do withdrawals take?**
Withdrawals are reviewed by the team before being processed. Processing times vary but you'll be notified by email when it's done.

**What is site credit?**
Site credit is non-withdrawable credit that can be used to buy competition tickets. It cannot be sent to a bank account. It appears separately from your cash balance in My Wallet.

**Is there a minimum withdrawal amount?**
Yes. The minimum withdrawal amount is shown when you open the withdrawal form in My Wallet.

---

## Account & Safe Play

**Can I change my email address?**
Yes, from **My Account**. Your new email must not already be registered on the platform.

**What is a spend cap?**
A spend cap limits how much you can spend within a rolling time period you choose. Once you hit the limit, ticket purchases are blocked until the period resets.

**Can I remove a spend cap once set?**
You cannot remove a cap yourself — you can only lower it (which applies immediately) or raise it (which applies at the end of the current period). To remove a cap entirely, contact our support team.

**What happens during a cool-off?**
Your account is paused. You cannot enter competitions or make purchases. Only our support team can lift a cool-off early.

**Can I hide my name from the winners list?**
Yes. Toggle **Hide Identity** on in your Safe Play settings. Your display name will be shown instead of your full name on all public-facing lists.

## Technical

**The page isn't loading correctly — what should I do?**
Try clearing your browser cache and reloading. If the problem continues, contact our support team with details of your browser and what you were trying to do.

**I'm not receiving emails — what should I do?**
Check your spam or junk folder. If they're not there, make sure the email address on your account is correct under **My Account**.
