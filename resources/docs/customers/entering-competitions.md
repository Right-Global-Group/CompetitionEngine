# Entering Competitions

> How to browse, pick tickets and enter competitions on Competition Engine.

---

## Browsing Competitions

The homepage and competitions page show all currently live draws. Each competition card displays the prize name, ticket price, how many tickets have been sold, a countdown to the draw, and an Enter Now button.

Use the category filters at the top of the competitions page to narrow down by prize type. Competitions can also be sorted by ending soonest, newest, or price.

---

## Competition Page

Clicking a competition opens its dedicated page. From here you can read the full prize description, see the gallery, check how many tickets remain, and choose how many to enter.

### Ticket Price

The ticket price is shown in the top right of the entry panel. If the competition has a sale price active, the original price is shown with a strikethrough alongside the discounted price.

### Countdown

The timer shows days, hours, minutes and seconds remaining until the competition ends. The progress bar shows how many tickets have been sold as a percentage of the total pool.

---

## Selecting Tickets

Use the slider or the +/− buttons to choose how many tickets you want. You can also type a number directly into the quantity field. Quick-pick buttons may appear below the slider with common multibuy quantities.

Some competitions have a **minimum ticket** requirement — you must add at least that many tickets. A notice appears above the entry panel if this applies.

Some competitions have a **maximum tickets per order** limit — you cannot add more than this in a single order. If you already have tickets for this competition in your basket, the remaining allowance is shown.

Some competitions have a **maximum tickets per customer** limit across all orders. Once you have reached that limit the entry panel is replaced with a message confirming you hold the maximum.

### Pick Your Number

Some competitions let you choose your own ticket numbers instead of being assigned them randomly. In this mode a number picker replaces the slider. Select your preferred numbers from those still available. Reserved numbers are held for 5 minutes — a countdown appears at checkout to show when your reservation expires.

---

## Multibuy Discounts

If the competition has multibuy tiers, the quick-pick buttons show the discount at each level. The total price updates automatically as you change quantity to reflect any discount applied.

---

## Golden Ticket

Some competitions offer an optional Golden Ticket upgrade. When available, a toggle appears in the entry panel below the ticket selector. Enabling it adds a percentage surcharge to your order total, and if your ticket is drawn as the winner you receive a bonus cash prize on top of the main prize.

The toggle shows the bonus amount and the extra cost. It can be turned on or off before adding to basket. Some competitions have Golden Ticket enabled by default.

---

## Free Postal Entry

Every competition includes a free postal entry route as required by UK competition law. Click the **Free Postal Entry** tab in the entry panel to see the full instructions, including what to write on the postcard and the address to send it to.

---

## Adding to Basket

Click **Get Tickets Now** to add your selection to the basket. A success message confirms the tickets have been added. The basket icon in the header updates to reflect your new quantity.

You can continue browsing and add tickets from multiple competitions before checking out.

---

## Time Locks and Restrictions

Some competitions are locked during certain hours of the day. If a time lock is active, the entry panel shows when the competition unlocks and the buttons are disabled until then.

Some competitions require a waiting period between orders from the same customer. If you are in a cooldown period, a countdown shows how long until you can purchase again.
