# Refer a Friend

> Share your link, earn a reward when a friend makes their first purchase.

---

## Overview

Refer a Friend gives you a personal link to share. When someone signs up through your link and goes on to make a qualifying purchase, you can both earn a reward.

{% hint style="info" %}
Refer a Friend may not be available on every site — it's an optional programme each site can switch on. If you don't see it in **My Account**, it isn't running here.
{% endhint %}

---

## Finding your link

Go to **My Account → Refer a Friend**. You may also see it promoted as a banner elsewhere on the site, or in a pop-up after completing an order.

Your page shows:

- Your personal referral link, with a **Copy** button.
- Share buttons for WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat, Facebook, Messenger, Telegram, X, Reddit, LinkedIn, SMS and Email.
- Your stats: clicks on your link, people referred, conversions, and total earned.
- A history of everyone you've referred, with their status and what you earned from each.

Most share buttons open that app with your link ready to send. For Instagram, TikTok and Snapchat — which don't support pre-filled links — a ready-made message and your link are copied to your clipboard so you can paste them in yourself.

---

## Accepting terms

Before your link and code are shown, you'll be asked to accept the Refer a Friend terms and conditions — a one-time checkbox. Until you accept, your page shows a preview only; your actual link isn't revealed.

Your friend does **not** need to accept anything — once they sign up through your link, everything on their side happens automatically.

---

## How it works

1. **Share your link.** Anyone who clicks it is remembered for a short time, even if they don't sign up immediately.
2. **They sign up.** As soon as they create an account through your link, you'll get an email letting you know.
3. **They make a qualifying purchase.** A qualifying purchase is a paid order (card or PayPal) — free entries and orders paid entirely with wallet balance don't count. Some sites also require a minimum spend before a referral qualifies.
4. **Rewards are paid.** Once your friend's purchase qualifies, both of you may receive a reward — the amount and type (cash or site credit) depends on the site's programme. Sometimes this happens instantly; sometimes there's a short wait before it's released, or it's reviewed by the team first. Either way, you'll get an email as soon as your reward is credited to your wallet.

{% hint style="info" %}
Cash rewards go through the same checks as any other cash in your wallet, including a short holding period before they can be withdrawn.
{% endhint %}

---

## If an order is refunded

If your friend's qualifying order is refunded before your reward has been paid out, the referral is cancelled and no reward is paid. If your reward had already been paid out, it stays with you — the team will review the situation rather than removing anything automatically.

---

## Referral status

Your referral history shows each friend's current status:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | They've signed up but haven't made a qualifying purchase yet. |
| **Awaiting Approval** | Their purchase qualified and is being reviewed by the team. |
| **Held** | Their purchase qualified and your reward is in a short waiting period before release. |
| **Rewarded** | Your reward has been paid. |
| **Rejected** | The referral didn't go through — for example the order was refunded, or it didn't pass the site's checks. |

---

## Good to know

- You can't refer yourself, and an account can only be credited as someone's referral once.
- Rewards are tied to real, paid purchases — orders made entirely with wallet balance, or free entries, don't trigger a reward.
- If you're subject to any account restrictions on the site, this may affect your ability to earn a referral reward.
