# Your Account

> Everything you can manage from your account area once you're logged in.

---

## My Account

The main account page lets you update your personal details. All fields are required.

| Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| **First Name** | Your legal first name. |
| **Last Name** | Your legal last name. |
| **Email** | Must be unique — no two accounts can share an email address. |
| **Phone** | Your mobile number. |
| **Date of Birth** | Required for age verification purposes. Format: DD/MM/YYYY. |

Click **Save** to apply changes.

### Changing Your Password

Click **Change Password** to open the password modal. You need to enter your current password, then your new password twice. Passwords must be at least medium strength — they need to contain letters and numbers. Adding uppercase letters and special characters makes the password strong. Weak passwords (letters or numbers only, under 6 characters) are rejected.

The strength indicator updates as you type. The **Update** button stays disabled until both fields match and the password meets minimum strength.

## My Addresses

The addresses page shows your saved billing and shipping addresses side by side. Click **Edit** on either card to open the address form.

### Address fields

| Field | Required |
|-------|----------|
| Address Line 1 | Yes |
| Address Line 2 | No |
| Address Line 3 | No |
| City | Yes |
| County | No |

Billing and shipping addresses are stored separately and can be different. Your billing address is used at checkout; the shipping address is used when a physical prize is being sent to you.

## My Tickets

A list of all competition tickets you hold. You can filter between active competitions (still running) and past competitions (ended or drawn). Each entry shows the competition name, your ticket numbers, and the draw date.

Clicking into a competition shows your individual ticket numbers for that entry, paginated 100 per page.

## My Wallet

Your wallet holds two types of balance — cash and site credit — shown on separate tabs.

### Cash Balance

Cash is withdrawable. It comes from prizes won as cash alternatives, wallet deposits, and cashback rewards. Your cash balance is shown at the top of the Cash tab.

**Depositing** — click **Deposit** to top up your wallet by card. The deposit goes through the checkout flow and is confirmed once payment is approved.

**Withdrawing** — click **Withdraw** to request a bank transfer. You need to provide:

- Your UK sort code (6 digits)
- Your UK account number (8 digits)

Withdrawals are reviewed by the team before being processed. A minimum withdrawal amount applies. Once submitted, the withdrawal appears in your transaction list as Pending until it is approved or rejected.

### Site Credit

Site credit is non-withdrawable. It can be used to pay for competition tickets at checkout but cannot be transferred to a bank account. It appears on the Site Credit tab when the feature is active on the platform.

Site credit comes from instant win prizes claimed as credit, or from deposit bonuses where applicable.

### Transaction History

Both tabs show a paginated transaction list. Each row shows the transaction type, date, and amount. Credits are shown in green, debits in red. Pending transactions are shown with strikethrough formatting until they clear.

Transaction types you may see:

| Type | What it means |
|------|--------------|
| **Deposit** | Money added to your wallet by card. |
| **Withdraw** | Money sent to your bank account. |
| **Winnings** | Cash prize credited from a competition win or instant win. |
| **Spend** | Ticket purchase deducted from your wallet. |
| **Cashback** | Cashback bonus earned on an order. |
| **Site Credit** | Non-withdrawable credit from a prize claim or deposit bonus. |
| **Split Payment** | A purchase where your wallet covered part of the total and card covered the rest. Expand the row to see the full breakdown. |

## My Safe Play

Safe Play lets you set responsible gambling limits on your account. There are three controls: a spend cap, a cool-off break, and an identity toggle.

### Spend Cap

Set a maximum amount you are willing to spend within a rolling period. You can choose from daily, weekly, fortnightly, monthly, or a custom number of days.

Use the slider or type directly into the amount field to set your cap between £0 and £1,000. Quick-pick buttons (£0, £10, £25, £50, £100, £250, £500) let you set common values in one click.

**£0** is a valid cap — it blocks all spending entirely.

A confirmation modal shows your chosen cap and reset date before saving. Once saved, the cap locks until the end of the current period. You cannot change it again until that period ends — this prevents raising your cap impulsively during a session.

If you want to increase your cap, the increase is scheduled to take effect at the end of the current period. You can cancel a pending increase at any time before it applies.

If you want to decrease your cap, the decrease applies immediately.

### Cool-Off (Take a Break)

A cool-off pauses your account for a set duration. During a cool-off you cannot enter competitions or make any purchases. Choose a duration from the slider:

- 1 day
- 1 week
- 1 month
- 3 months
- 1 year
- Permanent

A confirmation modal warns you that once started, only the support team can lift a cool-off early. Permanent cool-offs can only ever be lifted by contacting support.

While on a cool-off, the page shows a countdown ring with the remaining time.

### Hide Identity

Toggle this on to hide your full name from the public winners list, competition ticket lists, and the homepage winners feed. When hidden, you appear as your display name instead of your full name. The toggle takes effect immediately.

### Support Links

The Safe Play page links to GamCare (0808 8020 133), BeGambleAware, Samaritans (116 123), and Citizens Advice for anyone who needs to talk to someone.

## Claim Prizes

The Claim Prizes page is where you action any physical or alternative prizes you have won. Prizes appear here when you have won an instant win that offers a choice between the physical item, a cash alternative, or site credit.

Each prize card shows the prize name, competition, and date won. The options available depend on how the prize was configured:

| Option | What happens |
|--------|-------------|
| **Cash** | The cash value is credited to your wallet as withdrawable winnings immediately. |
| **Site Credit** | The credit value is added to your site credit balance immediately (non-withdrawable). |
| **Physical Prize** | A shipping request is submitted. The team will contact you to arrange delivery. |

Not all options are available for every prize — the admin configures which claim types apply to each prize category.

Main draw prizes (competition winners) also appear here when you have been notified by the team. The same three options apply, subject to what was configured for that competition.

Once claimed in any form, the prize is removed from this page.

## ID Verification

The ID Verification page lets you submit a photo of your ID for age verification. This is required on some platforms before you can claim prizes or make withdrawals.

### Submitting your ID

Click the upload area and select a photo of a valid ID document — a driving licence, passport, or national ID card. The document must clearly show your date of birth. Any common image format is accepted (JPG, PNG, HEIC, etc). The image is automatically compressed before upload.

The verification statuses are:

| Status | What it means |
|--------|--------------|
| **Not submitted** | Upload your document to begin. |
| **Pending** | Your document has been submitted and is awaiting review. Usually completed within 24 hours. |
| **Verified** | Your age has been confirmed. |
| **Rejected** | Your submission was not approved. The rejection reason is shown and you can resubmit a clearer photo. |

You will receive an email notification at each stage. Once verified, the verified badge is shown permanently and no further action is needed.
