# Basket & Checkout

> Managing your basket and completing a purchase.

---

## Your Basket

The basket holds your selected tickets before you pay. Click the basket icon in the header to view it. Each item shows the competition name, the quantity selected, any multibuy discount applied, and the line total.

### Updating quantities

You can increase or decrease the quantity for any item in the basket, subject to the competition's minimum and maximum ticket limits. Changing the quantity recalculates the total immediately.

### Removing items

Click the remove button on any item to take it out of the basket. The basket total updates automatically.

### Competitions ending soon

If a competition in your basket ends before you check out, it is automatically removed when you reach the checkout page. A warning message explains which items were removed.

---

## Applying a Coupon

If you have a coupon code, enter it in the coupon field in the basket. The discount is applied immediately and shown as a line below the subtotal. Coupons can be a fixed amount off or a percentage. Some coupons are restricted to specific competitions or require a minimum basket value.

---

## Checkout

When you're ready to pay, click **Checkout**. You must be logged in — if not you'll be directed to the login page first.

### Order summary

The checkout page shows a summary of your basket items, any discount applied, and the final total.

### Payment methods

You can pay by card, PayPal, or from your wallet balance (if you have one). If your wallet balance covers part but not all of the total, you can use a split payment — your wallet covers as much as it can and the remainder is charged to card or PayPal.

**Card payment** — enter your card details directly in the payment form. 3D Secure authentication may be required depending on your bank.

**PayPal** — you are redirected to PayPal to authorise the payment, then returned to the site.

**Wallet** — if your balance covers the full total, no card details are needed. The amount is deducted from your balance when the order is confirmed.

**Split payment** — your full wallet balance is applied automatically and you pay the remaining amount by card or PayPal.

### Billing address

Your saved billing address is used at checkout. If you haven't saved one yet, you'll be prompted to add it before completing the order. You can update your billing address from the My Addresses section of your account.

---

## Order Confirmation

Once payment is approved you're taken to the order confirmation page. Your tickets are assigned at this point. You'll also receive a confirmation email.

If payment fails for any reason you are taken to an error page with the reason. You can return to checkout and try again.

---

## Free Entry at Checkout

If you have a free entry assignment for a competition, you can use it at checkout. Free entry items show a £0 total in the basket and no payment is taken for those tickets. You can mix free entry and paid tickets in the same order.

---

## Checkout Flow Summary

```
Browse competitions
       ↓
Add tickets to basket
       ↓
Apply coupon (optional)
       ↓
Proceed to checkout (login required)
       ↓
Select payment method
       ↓
Enter payment details / authorise
       ↓
Order confirmed → tickets assigned
       ↓
Confirmation page + email
```
