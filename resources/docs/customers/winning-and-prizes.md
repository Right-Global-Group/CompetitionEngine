# Winning & Prizes

> Instant wins, main draw prizes, and how to claim your winnings.

---

## Instant Wins

Instant wins are prizes triggered during a competition entry. When you purchase tickets for a competition that has instant wins enabled, your tickets are checked against the instant win pool at the point your order is confirmed. If one of your tickets is a winner, you are notified immediately on the order confirmation page and by email.

Instant wins are distributed randomly across the ticket pool when the competition is published. The prizes are set by the admin and can be anything from cash amounts to physical items.

### Instant win prize types

Some instant win prizes are paid to your wallet automatically as withdrawable cash when your order is confirmed. Others require you to choose how you'd like to receive the prize — these appear in **Claim Prizes** in your account.

---

## Main Draw Prizes

The main draw happens when a competition ends. The winning ticket is selected and the winner is announced. If you win the main draw, the team will contact you by email and notification to let you know.

Main prizes are managed through the **Claim Prizes** page in your account once the team has sent you a notification. Until you receive that notification, the prize will not appear there.

---

## Claiming Prizes

Prizes that require action on your part appear in **My Account → Claim Prizes**. Each prize card shows the prize name, the competition it came from, and the date you won.

The options available to you depend on how the prize was configured. Not every option is available for every prize.

| Option | What happens |
|--------|-------------|
| **Cash** | The cash value is credited to your wallet immediately as withdrawable winnings. You can then withdraw it to your bank account. |
| **Site Credit** | The credit value is added to your site credit balance immediately. It can be used to buy more tickets but cannot be withdrawn. |
| **Physical Prize** | A shipping request is submitted. The team will contact you to arrange delivery of the physical item. |

Once you choose an option the prize is removed from the list and cannot be changed.

### Prize claiming flow

```
Win a prize (instant win or main draw)
           ↓
Prize appears in Claim Prizes page
           ↓
Choose how to claim:
  ├── Cash → credited to wallet immediately
  ├── Site Credit → added to site credit balance immediately
  └── Physical Prize → team contacts you to arrange shipping
           ↓
Prize marked as claimed and removed from list
```

---

## Winners List

Recent winners are displayed on the homepage in a scrolling ticker and on the winners page. Your name appears on the winners list when you win a draw or an instant win. If you'd rather not be named publicly, you can enable **Hide Identity** in your Safe Play settings — your display name will be shown instead of your full name.

---

## Withdrawing Winnings

Cash prizes credited to your wallet are withdrawable. Go to **My Wallet → Withdraw** to request a bank transfer. See the [Wallet](wallet) page for full withdrawal instructions.
