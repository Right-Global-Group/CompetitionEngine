# ID Verification

> Why we verify your age and how to complete it.

---

## Why We Ask for ID

Age verification confirms you are over 18. It is required before you can claim prizes or make a withdrawal on platforms where age verification is enabled.

---

## How to Submit Your ID

Go to **My Account → ID Verification**. Click the upload area and select a photo of a valid ID document. Accepted documents are:

- Driving licence
- Passport
- National ID card

The document must clearly show your date of birth. Any common image format is accepted — JPG, PNG, HEIC, and others. There is no strict file size limit as the image is automatically compressed before it is sent.

Once you have selected your file and confirmed it looks correct, click **Submit for Verification**.

---

## What Happens Next

Your document is reviewed by the team. This usually takes less than 24 hours. You will receive an email at each stage of the process.

| Status | What it means |
|--------|--------------|
| **Not submitted** | No document has been uploaded yet. |
| **Pending** | Your document has been submitted and is awaiting review. |
| **Verified** | Your age has been confirmed. No further action needed. |
| **Rejected** | Your submission was not approved. The reason is shown on the page and you can submit a new photo. |

---

## If Your ID is Rejected

The rejection reason is shown on the ID Verification page. Common reasons include the photo being blurry, the date of birth not being clearly visible, or the document being expired. Upload a new, clearer photo of the same or a different accepted document to resubmit.

---

## After Verification

Once verified, a green Verified badge is shown on the ID Verification page permanently. You will not need to submit ID again. You can now claim prizes and make withdrawals (if applicable on your platform).
