# Wallet

> Your wallet balance, deposits, withdrawals, and transaction history.

---

## Overview

Your wallet has two separate balances — cash and site credit. They are shown on separate tabs in **My Wallet**.

| Balance type | Can be withdrawn | Can buy tickets | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cash** | Yes | Yes | Deposits, prize winnings, cashback |
| **Site Credit** | No | Yes | Prize claims as site credit, deposit bonuses |

---

## Cash Balance

Your cash balance is withdrawable. It can be used to pay for tickets at checkout or sent to your bank account via a withdrawal request.

### Depositing

Click **Deposit** on the Cash tab to top up your wallet. Enter the amount and complete the card payment through the checkout. Once the payment is approved the funds appear in your balance immediately.

There is a minimum and maximum deposit amount per transaction.

### Withdrawing

Click **Withdraw** on the Cash tab to request a bank transfer. You need to provide:

- Your UK sort code (6 digits, no dashes)
- Your UK account number (8 digits)

The sort code and account number must be valid UK banking formats. The name on the bank account should match your Competition Engine account name.

A minimum withdrawal amount applies. Withdrawal requests are reviewed by the team before being paid out. Once submitted the request appears in your transaction list as **Pending**. When processed it moves to **Completed**. If declined it shows as **Rejected**.

---

## Site Credit

Site credit is non-withdrawable and shown on the Site Credit tab. It works like cash for buying tickets but cannot be sent to a bank account. The balance is clearly labelled as non-withdrawable.

Site credit can come from instant win prizes that you chose to claim as credit, or from deposit bonuses where applicable.

---

## Transaction History

Both tabs show a paginated list of your transactions, most recent first.

| Transaction type | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Deposit** | Money added to your wallet |
| **Withdraw** | Money sent to your bank account |
| **Winnings** | Cash prize from an instant win or draw |
| **Spend** | Wallet funds used toward a ticket purchase |
| **Cashback** | Bonus earned on an order |
| **Site Credit** | Non-withdrawable credit added to your account |
| **Split Payment** | Purchase where wallet and card both contributed |

Credits show in green, debits in red. Pending transactions show with a strikethrough until they clear. Rejected transactions are also struck through.

### Split payment breakdown

If an order was paid using a combination of wallet and card, it shows as a **Split Payment** in the list. Expand the row to see the wallet portion, the card portion, and the total paid.

### Pagination

Use the **Previous** and **Next** buttons at the bottom of the transaction list to move between pages.

---

## Site Credit in Detail

Site credit is a non-withdrawable balance that works alongside your cash balance. It can be used to pay for competition tickets at checkout but cannot be transferred to a bank account.

### Where site credit comes from

| Source | How it works |
|--------|-------------|
| **Physical prize claim** | When you win a physical instant win prize and choose to take site credit instead, the credit value is added to your balance immediately. |
| **Deposit bonus** | If a deposit cashback promotion is active, a percentage of your deposit is credited as site credit when your deposit is confirmed. |
| **Instant win prize** | Some instant win categories award site credit directly rather than withdrawable cash. |

### Using site credit at checkout

Site credit is applied automatically when you pay by wallet. If you have both site credit and cash, site credit is drawn down first. You can also use site credit as the wallet portion of a split payment — site credit covers what it can and you pay the remaining amount by card. Site credit only applies through the wallet payment path.

### Site credit vs cash

| Feature | Cash | Site Credit |
|---------|------|-------------|
| Use to buy tickets | ✓ | ✓ |
| Withdraw to bank | ✓ | ✗ |
| Counts toward wallet balance | ✓ | Shown separately |
| Used first in checkout | No | Yes (if both present) |
