# Safe Play

> Spend limits, taking a break, and responsible gambling tools.

---

## Overview

The Safe Play page gives you tools to manage your spending and take breaks from the platform. All settings are found under **My Account → My Safe Play**.

The page shows a live summary of your current protections at the top — your active spend cap, whether you're on a cool-off, and your identity visibility.

---

## Spend Cap

A spend cap limits how much you can spend within a rolling period. Once you hit the cap, purchases are blocked until the period resets.

### Setting your cap

Use the slider or type directly into the amount field to choose a cap between £0 and £1,000. Quick-pick buttons for common amounts (£0, £10, £25, £50, £100, £250, £500) let you set a value in one click.

**£0 is a valid cap** — it blocks all spending entirely until the period resets.

Choose a reset period using the period chips below the slider:

| Period | How it works |
|--------|-------------|
| **Daily** | Resets every 1 day |
| **Weekly** | Resets every 7 days |
| **Fortnight** | Resets every 14 days |
| **Month** | Resets every 30 days |
| **Custom** | Enter any number of days between 1 and 365 |

The page shows the date your next reset will fall on before you confirm.

A confirmation modal summarises your chosen cap and period before saving.

### Changing your cap

Once a cap is set it is locked for the duration of the current period. You cannot change it again until the period ends — this prevents raising your limit impulsively during a session. The page shows the exact date and time the cap unlocks.

**Decreasing your cap** applies immediately, even mid-period.

**Increasing your cap** is scheduled and takes effect at the end of the current period. A pending increase is shown in a banner at the top of the Safe Play page. You can cancel a pending increase at any time before it applies.

### Spend cap summary

```
First set cap → applies immediately
       ↓
Cap locks until period ends
       ↓
Period ends → cap resets, changes take effect
       ↓
New period begins with same cap value
```

## Cool-Off (Take a Break)

A cool-off temporarily pauses your account. During a cool-off you cannot enter competitions or make any purchases. It is designed for when you want to step away from the platform for a defined period.

### Starting a cool-off

Use the slider to choose a duration:

- 1 day
- 1 week
- 1 month
- 3 months
- 1 year
- Permanent

A confirmation modal warns you that once a cool-off is started it can only be lifted early by contacting our support team. Permanent cool-offs can only ever be removed by support.

Once active, a countdown ring on the Safe Play page shows the remaining time. The end date is shown so you know exactly when your account will be accessible again.

If you need support lifted urgently, [contact us](/contact).

## Hide Identity

The identity toggle controls whether your full name appears publicly on the site — on the winners list, competition ticket lists, and the homepage winners feed.

Toggle it **on** to show your display name instead of your full name. Toggle it **off** to show your full name. The change takes effect immediately across the site.
